About

A small tool, built for club events

CertifyX started as an internal tool to stop hand-editing the same certificate template every event season. Now it's open for any club on campus to use.

Why it exists

Every workshop, hackathon or seminar ends the same way: a spreadsheet of names and a designer stuck editing fifty certificates one by one. CertifyX replaces that with a form, a live preview, and a one-click PDF.

It's deliberately small — one clear job, done well, rather than a bloated events platform.

How it's built

  • UIStatic HTML/CSS/JS pages served by Express — no build step required.
  • APIA single /api/generate endpoint renders each certificate as a PDF with pdfkit.
  • ConfigClub links and socials live in one file, server/data/config.js.
